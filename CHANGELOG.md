# Changelog

## 1.7.0
First public release.

- Personal plans: sign up, then paste an AI-generated plan or build your own
  workout week.
- Points, levels and 20 badges across workouts, food, water, sleep and runs.
- Web-radar Home, redesigned screens (matte black, IBM Plex).
- Web-shot effects and sounds, with a mute switch.
- Custom food entry, weekly weigh-in, progress charts.
- Fully local: no account and no network permission.
